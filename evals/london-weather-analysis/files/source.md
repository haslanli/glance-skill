## 78 years of Heathrow weather: what the data shows

**Short version:** Heathrow has warmed by about **1.9 °C** in daytime highs since the 1950s, and night-time lows rose by about the same. Frost days have roughly halved. Rainfall shows no clear trend.

Data: Met Office monthly records for the Heathrow station, **1948–2025** (**936** months). The decade table starts in 1950.

### Average daily high and low, by decade

| Decade | Tmax | Tmin |
|---|---|---|
| 1950s | 14.2 °C | 6.3 °C |
| 1960s | 14.0 °C | 6.4 °C |
| 1970s | 14.3 °C | 6.7 °C |
| 1980s | 14.4 °C | 6.6 °C |
| 1990s | 15.1 °C | 7.3 °C |
| 2000s | 15.4 °C | 7.7 °C |
| 2010s | 15.6 °C | 7.8 °C |
| 2020s (to 2025) | 16.1 °C | 8.3 °C |

### Trends (linear fit, 1948–2025)

- Tmax: **+0.21 °C** per decade. Tmin: **+0.24 °C** per decade.
- Air frost days: **46** a year on average in the 1950s, **21** in the 2010s.
- Sunshine: **1,410** hours a year in the 1950s, **1,560** in the 2010s, about **11 %** more.
- Rainfall: **612 mm** a year in the 1950s, **628 mm** in the 2010s. That change is far smaller than the year-to-year spread (standard deviation **104 mm**), so it isn't a trend.

### How the measures move together (monthly correlation)

- Tmax and tmin: **0.97**
- Tmax and sunshine: **0.83**
- Tmax and frost days: **−0.75**
- Rain and sunshine: **−0.25**
- Rain and tmax: **−0.05** (no relationship)

### Extremes

- Warmest year: **2022**, mean tmax **17.3 °C**.
- Coldest year: **1963**, mean tmin **4.1 °C**.

### Limits

- Monthly means hide heatwaves and cold snaps; daily data would be needed for those.
- The area around Heathrow has been built up since 1948, so part of the warming may be local (an urban heat island) rather than regional.
- A straight-line trend is a simplification: most of the rise happened after **1980**.
